# About us

**Queer Hangout** is a social arena for Queer adults in Drammen and a volunteer organisation that meets once a month. We also have activities like sauna, christmas workshops, forest walks or breakfast on the 17th of may.

Queer Hangout is not just a friend group, but also a group of activists who work against loneliness in the queer community. For example, a lot of disabled people cannot participate in the queer community due to lacking accessibility. For these people, we have therefore built our own wheelchair ramp and chosen accessible locales with handicap toilets. Our goal is that everyone can participate! We work with each member and relevant organisations, so that things are done right.

## What we have achieved

Since Queer Hangout was created on February 2022, we have arranged:

### “Skeiv Dag” (Queer day) at Tollbugata Street

Despite there being several queer organizations in Drammen, we don't often talk to each other, as we have different demographies. Therefore, Queer Hangout startet “Skeiv Dag på Tollbugata” (Queer Day at Tollbugata Street), a day where the queer organizations are invited to bring their own stands and activities, and share it with the rest of Drammen. There was a small market, food, jewelry workshop and a clown that made balloon animals! The goal was to build queer solidarity in Drammen, and just generally have a good time! The event was financially supported by the Strømsø 2030 initiative.

### Fun on the beach

Picnic, kubb, frisbee, music and bathing. 2 years in a row, Queer Hangout has gone to the beach!

### A meeting every month!

Queer Hangout, then called Skeiv Hobbykveld, started on Drammen library February 2022. Since August 2022, we have been at Criollo choclatebar. For a period, we were meeting twice a month, and the second event was held at Strømsø knutepunkt. Now that Criollo is no longer open, we meet at Kometen Pizza&Bar.

![A room with comfortable chairs, colorful decorations and plants hanging from the celing.](/images/regnskogrommet.jpg "Photo taken by Odd Arild Ringen")

### Building a wheelchair ramp

We might have a low budget, but we compensate by volunteer work. We have built wheelchair ramps whenever they were needed, made of wood and plywood.

![A person screwing screws into a homemade wheelchair ramp.](/images/rullestolrampe.jpg "Anonymous photographer, used with permission")

### Queer retro ball

During Drammen Pride 2023, Queer Hangout made a Queer Ball at Strømsø Knutepunkt. It had music, a photobooth, free soda, snacks and a lot of balloons.

![Two people holding around each other and posing for the camera. They are in the photobooth.](/images/retroball.jpg "Anonymous photographer, used with permission")

### Money drive for Gender affirming surgery

On multiple markets, Queer Hangout worked together to collect and sell used clothing. It was to collect money for a members gender affirming surgery. 
If you're able to read Norwegian, you can read about this and about gender affirming surgeries in general by [clicking here.](/no/innsamling)
