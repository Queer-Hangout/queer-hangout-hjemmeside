## Sending a complaint or warning

If you have experienced something uncomfortable at Queer Hangout, you can mail us at [varsel@queerhangout.no](mailto:varsel@queerhangout.no). This will only be seen by the leader for Queer Hangout, and they will handle it as discreetly as possible.

If your issue is with the leadership of Queer Hangout, you can go to [FRI's warning page.](https://www.foreningenfri.no/varsling-2/) FRI is our “mother organization”, and no members of Queer Hangout will see what you send them.
