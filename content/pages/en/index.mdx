# Welcome to Queer Hangout!

**Queer Hangout** is a social arena for Queer adults in Drammen and a volunteer organisation that meets once a month. We also have activities like sauna, christmas workshops, forest walks or breakfast on the 17th of may.

Queer Hangout is not just a friend group, but also a group of activists who work against loneliness in the queer community. For example, a lot of disabled people cannot participate in the queer community due to lacking accessibility. For these people, we have therefore built our own wheelchair ramp and chosen accessible locales with handicap toilets. Our goal is that everyone can participate! We work with each member and relevant organisations, so that things are done right.
