# Our smaller and larger goals

![Six people are having a picnic, sitting on blankets by the Drammen river.](/images/piknik.jpg "Photo taken by Leona Imperatori")

Queer Hangout is filled with “fixers”, so we can do a lot with little. Everything, from this webpage to wheelchair ramps, have been made by someone on Queer Hangout on volunteer basis. And yet, there is a lot we cannot do without money, so we appreciate any donation we get! Here's our current goals: 

## Naturegroup

We wish to make a queer walk- and naturegroup. We wish to invest in cookingware for trips, fishing rods, and anything else you could need for nature activities.

## Renting a pool or sauna

We have a large group of transpeople who do not feel safe in gendered changing rooms and swimming halls. Many experience that, no matter what gendered wardrobe they choose, there is someone who is angry or afraid. POGI (Norways Patientorganization for Gender Incogruens) has once rented an entire pool and sauna just for transpeople, and this has been a huge success! We wish to do the same in Drammen.

## Courses in signlanguage.

We want as many people to be able to join, and we want to invite groups that are rarely made accessible for. One of these groups are deaf people, so we wish to have a courses in sign language for the members of Queer Hangout. This goal might change if it turns out there is better ways to make it accessible to deaf people.
